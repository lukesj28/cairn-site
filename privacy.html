Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

This page covers the cairnstacks.app website. For what the Cairn app itself can access, see Privacy & Permissions in the docs.

No cookies, anonymous counts

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It counts anonymous, aggregate usage with Umami Cloud: page views and clicks such as downloads, helper topics and cairn stones. Nothing you type into the helper is sent to Umami, only a broad topic like “installation”.

The helper chat

When you ask the helper a question, your message and up to three earlier exchanges are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce a reply. Replies may be AI-generated. This site doesn’t store or log your messages. Please don’t enter personal information.

Your IP address

Your IP address is used only to rate-limit the helper per minute, through Cloudflare. This site doesn’t store it.

Hosting

The site and its videos are served by Cloudflare (cairnstacks.app and cdn.cairnstacks.app), which processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages.

Ko-fi

Ko-fi only loads when you open the donate panel. From then on, Ko-fi’s privacy policy applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/lukesj28/cairn/issues.
